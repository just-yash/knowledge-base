# Coordinate Geometry (LEEE)

---

# BASIC FORMULAS

## Distance Formula
- $$\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$

## Section Formula

- Internal division:
  $$\left(\frac{mx_2 + nx_1}{m+n}, \frac{my_2 + ny_1}{m+n}\right)$$

- Midpoint:
  $$\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$$

---

# SLOPE AND LINE

## Slope
- $$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$

## Equation of Line

- Slope-intercept:
  $$y = mx + c$$

- Point-slope:
  $$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$$

- Two-point form:
  $$\frac{y - y_1}{y_2 - y_1} = \frac{x - x_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$

---

# GENERAL FORM OF LINE

- $$Ax + By + C = 0$$

## Slope from general form
- $m = -\frac{A}{B}$

---

# ANGLE BETWEEN LINES

- $$\tan\theta = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{1 + m_1 m_2}$$

## Conditions

- Parallel:
  - $m_1 = m_2$

- Perpendicular:
  - $m_1 m_2 = -1$

---

# DISTANCE FROM POINT TO LINE

- $$\frac{|Ax + By + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$$

---

# INTERCEPT FORM

- $$\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1$$

---

# PAIR OF STRAIGHT LINES (BASIC)

- Homogeneous equation:
  $$ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$$

---

# CIRCLE

## Standard Equation
- $$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$$

## General Form
- $$x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$$

## Center and Radius
- Center: $(-g, -f)$  
- Radius: $$\sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$$

---

# IMPORTANT RESULTS (CIRCLE)

## Equation of diameter
- Midpoint of endpoints is center

## Tangent to circle
- $$xx_1 + yy_1 = r^2$$ (for circle at origin)

---

# POSITION OF POINT

- Substitute point in equation:
  - $< 0$ → inside  
  - $= 0$ → on circle  
  - $> 0$ → outside  

---

# PARABOLA

## Standard Equation
- $$y^2 = 4ax$$

## Key elements
- Focus: $(a, 0)$  
- Directrix: $x = -a$

---

# ELLIPSE (BASIC)

- $$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$$

---

# HYPERBOLA (BASIC)

- $$\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$$

---

# IMPORTANT RESULTS

## Length of tangent from point to circle
- $$\sqrt{S_1}$$
  where $S_1$ is value after substitution

---

## Condition of tangency
- Discriminant = 0

---

# TRANSFORMATION TRICKS

- Shift origin:
  - Replace $x \to x - h$, $y \to y - k$

---

# CORE FORMULAS SUMMARY

- Distance:
  $$\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$

- Line:
  $$y = mx + c$$

- Circle:
  $$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$$

- Distance to line:
  $$\frac{|Ax + By + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$$

- Parabola:
  $$y^2 = 4ax$$
