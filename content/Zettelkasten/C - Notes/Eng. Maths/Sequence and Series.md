# Sequence and Series 

---

# BASIC DEFINITIONS

- Sequence: ordered list → $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$
- Series: sum of sequence → $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots$

---

# ARITHMETIC PROGRESSION (AP)

## General term
- $$a_n = a + (n-1)d$$

## Sum of first n terms
- $$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$$

- Alternative form:
  $$S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$$

---

# IMPORTANT RESULTS (AP)

- $l = a + (n-1)d$

- Sum of first n natural numbers:
  $$\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

- Sum of squares:
  $$\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$$

- Sum of cubes:
  $$\left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^2$$

---

# GEOMETRIC PROGRESSION (GP)

## General term
- $$a_n = ar^{n-1}$$

## Sum of first n terms
- $$S_n = \frac{a(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}, \quad r \neq 1$$

## Infinite GP (|r| < 1)
- $$S_\infty = \frac{a}{1 - r}$$

---

# IMPORTANT RESULTS (GP)

- If $|r| < 1$, series converges  
- If $|r| \ge 1$, diverges  

---

# HARMONIC PROGRESSION (HP)

- Terms are reciprocals of AP  

Example:
- $\frac{1}{a}, \frac{1}{a+d}, \frac{1}{a+2d}$

---

# MEANS (IMPORTANT)

## Arithmetic Mean (AM)
- $$AM = \frac{a + b}{2}$$

## Geometric Mean (GM)
- $$GM = \sqrt{ab}$$

## Harmonic Mean (HM)
- $$HM = \frac{2ab}{a + b}$$

---

# RELATION BETWEEN MEANS

- $$AM \ge GM \ge HM$$

---

# IMPORTANT FORMULAS

## Insert k AMs between a and b

- Common difference:
  $$d = \frac{b - a}{k+1}$$

---

## Insert k GMs between a and b

- Common ratio:
  $$r = \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^{\frac{1}{k+1}}$$

---

# SPECIAL SERIES

## Sum of GP type
- $1 + r + r^2 + \dots = \frac{1}{1-r}$ (if $|r| < 1$)

---

## Telescoping series (pattern)
- Terms cancel out → simplify  

---

# IMPORTANT TRICKS (LEEE)

- Convert to known form:
  - AP / GP  

- Look for:
  - constant difference → AP  
  - constant ratio → GP  

- Use:
  - factorization  
  - cancellation  

---

- $$a_n = a + (n-1)d$$

- $$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$$

- $$a_n = ar^{n-1}$$

- $$S_n = \frac{a(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}$$

- $$S_\infty = \frac{a}{1 - r}$$
