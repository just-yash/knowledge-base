# Health Insurance
### Common Myths/Excuses 
- *"I'm healthy."*
    - Illness or accidents are unpredictable.
- *"I'm covered by family/work."*
    - This coverage is temporary. It ends if they (or you) lose their job, change policies, or retire.
- *"I can't afford it."*
    - If you can't afford the premium, you absolutely cannot afford a major medical bill.

### Checklist for Health Insurance
1. Adequate Coverage
	- Dont forget inflation and future needs
2. Pre-Existing Conditions
	- BE aware of waiting period of pre-existing conditions.
