# Burnt Petals

I held her like flowers to my chest,  
Was so addicted that I even forgot meth.  
A year later, I learned flowers have thorns,  
And every relationship has its pros and cons.

I hoped that she’s my forever woman,  
Thought that she would never abandon.  
A year later, I learned hoping is like shooting arrows in the dark,  
At first, you think there is a special spark.

I invested all my time and energy in her,  
Thought that I would never have my visions blurred.  
A year later, I learned these aren’t investments, they are bills,  
All the memories of her that are left with me kill.

---
### Reflection

This poem traces the journey from idealized love to painful awareness. It shows how devotion can quietly turn into debt, and how hope, once comforting, can become cruel. Beneath the heartbreak lies growth—the realization that not everything we pour ourselves into will give us warmth in return.
