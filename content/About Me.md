# About Me

I’m Yash Agrawall — a computer science engineering student interested in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and building systems that outlast short-term noise.

This site is my public Zettelkasten : a living knowledge base where I collect, connect, and refine ideas over time.

I use this space to:
- Think in public  
- Organize what I learn  
- Explore questions that don’t have neat answers  
- Write — both technically and creatively  

My learning philosophy is simple:  
understand fundamentals deeply, question assumptions relentlessly, and build from first principles.

Alongside technical notes, I also write poetry and reflections. Not as an escape from logic, but as its counterpart. Both are ways of observing reality.

I don’t claim expertise.  
I document process.

If you’re reading this, you’re looking at thoughts-in-progress.
