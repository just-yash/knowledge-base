# Supply Function and Supply Schedule

The supply function and supply schedule provide a formal and structured way to understand the relationships between the quantity of a commodity supplied and its various determinants.

---
## Supply Function

### Definition

The supply function expresses the functional relationship between the quantity supplied of a particular commodity and the factors that influence or determine it.

### 1) Individual Supply Function

- Focuses on the functional relationship between the supply of an individual firm and the factors affecting it.
    
- Expressed as: $$S_x = f(P_x, P_r, P_f, T, G, O)$$

Where:

- $S_x$: Quantity supplied of commodity $x$.
    
- $f$: Functional relationship.
    
- $P_x$: Price of the given commodity $x$.
    
- $P_r$: Prices of related (other) goods.
    
- $P_f$: Prices of factors of production (inputs).
    
- $T$: State of technology.
    
- $G$: Government policy (taxes and subsidies).
    
- $O$: Goals/Objectives of the firm.
    
### 2) Market Supply Function

- Represents the functional relationship between total market supply and the factors affecting it.
    
- Includes all individual factors plus market-specific determinants:
    
    $$S_M = f(P_x, P_r, P_f, T, G, O, N, E, F)$$
    
- Additional factors:
    
    - $N$: Number of firms in the market.
        
    - $E$: Future expectations regarding price($P_x$).
        
    - $F$: Infrastructure (Transport and Communication).

## Supply Schedule

### Definition

A supply schedule is a tabular statement showing various quantities of a commodity that producers are willing to supply at different price levels during a given period of time.

### 1) Individual Supply Schedule

- A table showing the quantities of a commodity that an **individual producer** is willing to sell at various prices.
    
- Demonstrates the **direct relationship** between price and quantity supplied.
    
###### Example

|**Price (₹)**|**Quantity Supplied by Firm A (Units)**|
|---|---|
|1|5|
|2|10|
|3|15|
|4|20|
|5|25|

### 2) Market Supply Schedule

- A table showing the total quantities of a commodity that **all producers** in the market are willing to sell at various prices.
    
- It is derived by the horizontal summation of individual supply schedules.

- Market Supply is the sum of all Individual Supplies in the Market

###### Example

Assumes a market with two firms: Firm A and Firm B.

|**Price (₹)**|**Firm A Supply (Units)**|**Firm B Supply (Units)**|**Market Supply (A + B)**|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|5|10|15|
|2|10|20|30|
|3|15|30|45|
|4|20|40|60|
|5|25|50|75|
