## 🌱 **PHASE 1 — Understanding Money (Weeks 1–4)**

**Goal:** Learn what money really _is_ and how personal finance works.  
You’ll understand saving, budgeting, banking, and the mindset of wealth.

🎥 **YouTube (Watch in this order):**

1. [What is Money? | Pranjal Kamra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BR0rj8D8RzE)
    
2. [Basics of Personal Finance | CA Rachana Ranade](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d5JStFQvYh8)
    
3. [Budgeting & Expense Tracking | Asset Yogi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QORLnlDkF4c)
    
4. [How Banks and Loans Work | Asset Yogi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=74uYp7F8SOQ)
    
5. [The Psychology of Money Summary | Pranjal Kamra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UeSRkP4xXKc)
    

📘 **Course:**

- [Zerodha Varsity – Personal Finance Module](https://zerodha.com/varsity/module/personal-finance/)
    

🪙 **Action:**

- Open a savings account (if not done).
    
- Create a simple expense tracker (Google Sheet or Notion).
    
- Save 10–20% of your monthly income (start small but be consistent).

## 💰 **PHASE 2 — Saving & Investing Basics (Months 2–3)**

**Goal:** Learn how to grow money safely and smartly.  
You’ll understand inflation, compounding, mutual funds, SIPs, and ETFs.

🎥 **YouTube:**

1. [Inflation, Interest, and Compounding | Pranjal Kamra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sGrpyFiqNEI)
    
2. [Mutual Funds Explained | CA Rachana Ranade](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T-nmNeW8AM4)
    
3. [How to Start SIPs | Asset Yogi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61uF2eSItbQ)
    
4. [Index Funds vs. Mutual Funds | Pranjal Kamra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPMpi4oN8L4)
    

📘 **Course:**

- [Zerodha Varsity – Investments Module](https://zerodha.com/varsity/module/introduction-to-investing/)
    

🪙 **Action:**

- Open a **Groww** or **Zerodha** account (for investing).
    
- Start a SIP (₹500–₹1000/month) in **Nifty 50 Index Fund.**
    
- Track your returns monthly (learn patience).

🎥 **YouTube:**

1. [Stock Market for Beginners | CA Rachana Ranade](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ufLHrncMYY)
    
2. [How the Stock Market Works | Pranjal Kamra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0W8cD5fQw58)

📘 **Course:**

- [Zerodha Varsity – Stock Markets Module](https://zerodha.com/varsity/module/introduction-to-stock-markets/)
    
- [Zerodha Varsity – Fundamental Analysis Module](https://zerodha.com/varsity/module/fundamental-analysis/)
    

🪙 **Action:**

- Create a **watchlist** of 5–10 companies.
    
- Use **Moneycontrol** or **Tickertape** to follow their stock trends.
    
- Read basic financial news daily (ET Markets or Finshots).

## 📊 **PHASE 4 — Trading (Months 7–9)**

**Goal:** Learn to earn short-term profits from market movements.

🎥 **YouTube:**

1. [Price Action & Candlestick Basics | Booming Bulls](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=03f8_JrLL5k)
    
2. [Technical Analysis Full Course | MarketGurukul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8jCqK7AKH8)
    
3. [Indicators & Charts | Trading with Vivek](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zb6zqPBFhCU)
    
4. [Risk Management for Traders | Pranjal Kamra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vEY5eTdf_w)
    

📘 **Course:**

- [Zerodha Varsity – Technical Analysis Module](https://zerodha.com/varsity/module/technical-analysis/)
    

🪙 **Action:**

- Practice **paper trading** using [Moneybhai](https://moneybhai.moneycontrol.com/) or [TradingView](https://in.tradingview.com/).
    
- Keep a **trading journal** — note entry, exit, reason, profit/loss.
    
- Risk only 1–2% of your capital per trade.
