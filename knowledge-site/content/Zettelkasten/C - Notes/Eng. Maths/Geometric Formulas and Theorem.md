# 2D GEOMETRY (AREA, PERIMETER, RESULTS)

## Square
- Area: $a^2$
- Perimeter: $4a$
- Diagonal: $a\sqrt{2}$

## Rectangle
- Area: $l \cdot b$
- Perimeter: $2(l + b)$
- Diagonal: $\sqrt{l^2 + b^2}$

## Triangle
- Area (basic): $$\frac{1}{2}bh$$
- Area (Heron): $A = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$, where $s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$

### Important Results
- Sum of angles = $180^\circ$
- Pythagoras theorem:
  $$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$

## Equilateral Triangle
- Area: $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2$
- Height: $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}a$

## Parallelogram
- Area: $b \cdot h$

## Circle
- Area: $$\pi r^2$$
- Circumference: $2\pi r$

### Important Results
- Diameter: $2r$
- Chord length: $2r\sin(\theta/2)$
- Equation of circle: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$

## Sector of Circle
- Area: $\frac{\theta}{2} r^2$
- Arc length: $\theta r$

# 3D GEOMETRY (VOLUME & SURFACE AREA)

## Cube
- Volume: $$a^3$$
- Surface Area: $6a^2$
- Diagonal: $a\sqrt{3}$

## Cuboid
- Volume: $$lbh$$
- Surface Area: $2(lb + bh + hl)$
- Diagonal: $\sqrt{l^2 + b^2 + h^2}$

## Sphere
- Volume: $$\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$
- Surface Area: $4\pi r^2$

## Hemisphere
- Volume: $\frac{2}{3}\pi r^3$
- Curved Surface Area: $2\pi r^2$
- Total Surface Area: $3\pi r^2$

## Cylinder
- Volume: $$\pi r^2 h$$
- Curved Surface Area: $2\pi r h$
- Total Surface Area: $2\pi r(h + r)$

## Cone
- Volume: $$\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$$
- Slant height: $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$
- Curved Surface Area: $\pi r l$
- Total Surface Area: $\pi r(l + r)$

## Frustum of Cone
- Volume: $$\frac{1}{3}\pi h (R^2 + r^2 + Rr)$$
- Curved Surface Area: $\pi (R + r) l$

# IMPORTANT THEOREMS & RELATIONS

## Similar Figures
- Ratio of areas = square of ratio of sides  
  $$
  \frac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\frac{a_1}{a_2}\right)^2
  $$

- Ratio of volumes = cube of ratio of sides  
  $$
  \frac{V_1}{V_2} = \left(\frac{a_1}{a_2}\right)^3
  $$

---

## Scaling Effect
- If dimensions scale by $k$:
  - Area scales by $k^2$
  - Volume scales by $k^3$

---

## Combination of Solids
- Add volumes:
  $V_{total} = V_1 + V_2$
- Subtract volumes for hollow shapes

---

## Common Transformations

### Cone from Cylinder
- Same base & height:
  $V_{cone} = \frac{1}{3} V_{cylinder}$

---

### Sphere inside Cube
- Cube side = $2r$
- Volume relation:
  $\frac{V_{sphere}}{V_{cube}} = \frac{\pi}{6}$

---

### Hemisphere + Cylinder
- Same radius & height:
  Useful for container problems

# COORDINATE GEOMETRY LINKS

## Distance Formula
$$\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$

## Midpoint Formula
$\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$
