# A Desolated Guy

I always find myself in a mess.  
In no time, I curse my existence.  
I’m done with hating myself every time.  
When will this phase come to an end?

I don’t deserve this kind of life.  
How about ending it before time?  
Continuously thinking about suicide.

I guess I’m depressed.  
When I think of the reason,  
There’s not one,  
But there’s a whole damn list.  
How about me ceasing to exist?

It’s difficult handling this pressure,  
And depression,  
Attending lectures,  
And saying, “Yes sir.”  
Got a rope of beautiful texture,  
How would its prints look on my neck,  
And later on my picture?

My mind’s out of place,  
Nothing is going great.  
I need someone to ease out the pain,  
But people are driving me insane.  
Honestly, I know they don’t care.

The world is too complicated for me to exist.  
Everybody’s a peck-sniff.  
People say something,  
And believe something else.  
Nobody’s got control over themselves.

Oh, I don’t know what to do, actually.  
Suffocating in this body,  
Bruises in my heart,  
Also have a wrecked mind,  
Trying to fight for my life.

There’s no one to save me.  
All my efforts go in vain.  
Not every ending is beaming,  
But the sooner the ending,  
The lesser will be the pain.

---
### Reflection
This poem captures the chaos of a mind overwhelmed by pressure, exhaustion, and intrusive thoughts. It doesn’t search for solutions—it simply documents what it feels like to exist inside the storm. Writing this is an act of honesty, a way of giving shape to pain instead of letting it remain silent. Even in its darkness, the poem stands as proof that the voice is still alive.
