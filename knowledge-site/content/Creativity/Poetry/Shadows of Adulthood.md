# Shadows of Adulthood

Life is so cruel, but I’m hanging along,  
Got friends, but I’m still feeling alone.  
I’m still going out wearing that cheap cologne,  
Going out and wanting to go back home.

I guess this is called adulting,  
Where you find bitching interesting,  
Where you are always on your phone,  
And still pretend to be busy with your daily chores.

Losing interest in everything you used to like,  
Wanting to leave everything and start a new life.  
Nothing seems to be of your type,  
Life’s so straight and monotype.

Where is the joyful kid who was excited for everything?  
Now nothing seems to be as fascinating.  
Sitting all alone and calling it chilling,  
Adulting is literally so boring.

Burdened by responsibilities,  
’Cause now you are not a teen.  
If you want to have a good life,  
Then ignore everything that comes in between.

---
### Reflection

This poem reflects the quiet fatigue that often accompanies growing up—the moment when excitement fades into obligation. It captures how adulthood can feel less like growth and more like slow erosion of wonder. Beneath the boredom and frustration is a longing to rediscover meaning beyond routine.
