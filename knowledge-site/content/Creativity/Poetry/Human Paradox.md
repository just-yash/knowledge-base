# Human Paradox

Fighting with myself in my mind,  
I don’t want to think what I’m thinking.  
Not following rules, didn’t even try,  
Hearing rumors that messed up my life.

I don’t know where to find peace,  
Just want to put my mind at ease.  
I don’t really understand this species,  
Looking at a camera and saying cheese.  
Investing in every other filth,  
Not realizing they are actually bills.

Why are humans so complicated?  
Why do they make filth overrated?  
They love their pets,  
And keep them domesticated.

Why can’t they be simple,  
And not follow every other ritual?  
Why do they have to make stupid rules,  
And waste all of their joules?

Setting up courts and laws,  
But not getting justice at all.  
Having police for our safety,  
But they don’t monitor every activity.

Doctors are on duty,  
But people are dying of acidity.  
Politicians are at work,  
Most of them are a bunch of jerks.  
Where are we heading this way?  
Being the hunter and also the prey.

Can we all please come together,  
And try to make things better?  
Build a sustainable future,  
Instead of abusing each other.

---
### Reflection

This poem questions the strange contradictions of human behavior—our intelligence alongside our cruelty, our systems alongside their failures. It captures the exhaustion of living in a world that claims progress while repeating the same mistakes. Beneath the frustration lies a simple hope: that humanity can choose cooperation over destruction.
