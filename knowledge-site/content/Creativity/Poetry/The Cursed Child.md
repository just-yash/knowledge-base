# The Cursed Child

I’m stuck in this house,  
I just need a way out.  
There is too much pressure,  
I guess I’m in depression.

My mum wants me to study science,  
And forget my personal life.  
They check my phone almost every day,  
Ignoring my state of mind.

They want me to be happy every time,  
Staying here every day and every night?  
I don’t know what they want from me,  
Agonizing me a hundred times.

You want the topper to be me,  
As if it’s no big deal.  
You ask me to do this and that,  
Don’t you ever feel bad?

You say that I waste money,  
Do you think that seriously?  
If that’s really the case,  
I think you’re better off without me.

You said that you would kill me  
If I don’t make it to the top three.  
C’mon, please try to understand,  
I’m a human, not a robot, Dad.

---
### Reflection

This poem voices the anguish of a child trapped between obedience and selfhood. It exposes how love becomes distorted when expectations outweigh empathy. Beneath the anger and hurt lies a simple desire: to be seen as human, not as a scoreboard.
