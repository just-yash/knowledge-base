# Strength in Solitude

You are nobody’s responsibility.  
You have no one for your mental stability.  
No one texts you just to check up on you;  
They text because they have some work for you.

The world wears a mask; it’s not like Disney.  
You have to be tough and let your softness fade swiftly.  
They ignore your texts and act all busy.  
You have to live alone, and I know it’s not easy.

You have to cage your feelings and live with all of this.  
Anything you say to anyone will be used against you.  
Everyone’s a snake, and in the end, they all will hiss.  
In the end, if there’s no one, you’ll always have you.

You cannot be naive and gullible.  
You cannot cry about it or be sentimental.  
You have to stand when the world pulls you down.  
Turn your weakness into strength and wear it like a crown.

---
### Reflection

This poem speaks from a place of hard-earned realism—where dependence fades and self-reliance becomes necessary. It doesn’t romanticize loneliness, but reframes it as a forge for strength. The message is simple yet powerful: when everything falls apart, the self becomes both the battlefield and the refuge.
