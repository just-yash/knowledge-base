# Beyond Now

Sometimes I miss you more than usual.  
I can’t see you for like a year, life is so cruel.  
Will everything turn out fine?  
Or will you just end up as my mutual?

There is nothing that I can do  
To see you every day and hold you,  
’Cause you are a thousand miles away,  
Sitting at the corner of a bay.

Looking at the stars,  
Missing you hard.  
Can you come back anytime soon,  
And stay with me as long as it lasts?

One thing about life is that it changes,  
We are breaking and making relations.  
Friends are turning into strangers,  
All you can find are abusers.

Two-faced people who want to use you completely,  
Won’t care about you and will harass you mentally.  
You do a lot of things under peer pressure,  
Substance abuse becomes a form of closure.

People change with time,  
I hope we never say goodbye.  
I have a lot of future plans,  
And I include you in all of them.


---
### Reflection

This poem expresses the ache of loving someone from a distance while fearing that time might quietly replace closeness with silence. It captures the tension between hope and inevitability—the desire to hold on even while recognizing how easily people drift apart. Beneath the longing lies a simple wish: to remain important in each other’s future, despite everything that changes.
