# My Best Friend

I miss you so badly,  
You were always there for me.  
The way you cared,  
You were my best friend.

You gave me all the happiness,  
You were so selfless.  
But I lost you in a second,  
You died from a heart attack.

Gazing at the celestial stars in the night sky,  
Wondering which star is my grandma.  
I wasn’t ready to say goodbye,  
Just wish to talk to you one last time.

Grandpa is so lonely.  
He waits for his time only.  
He sits alone on the chair,  
Staring at the empty pair.  
He won’t go out,  
Sits and cries in a corner of the house.

We had it all  
When we had you.  
Now we stare at the wall,  
’Cause it has a picture of you.

---
### Reflection

This poem captures grief in its quietest form—the small moments, the empty spaces, and the people left behind. It shows how love doesn’t disappear with death; it transforms into memory and longing. The simplicity of the words makes the loss feel deeply human and painfully real.
