# System

Starting my day at night,  
Working hard to get every question right.  
Also have a reduced appetite,  
Filling all of my terabytes.

I am so sleep-deprived,  
Practicing everything Newton derived.  
Never kept books out of my sight,  
Forgot about my hobbies and likes,  
Doing everything to get a good life.

Not complaining about the system,  
Of which every student is a victim.  
Increasing numbers of suicide cases,  
Of students who were fragile.

They don’t understand the pressure,  
They don’t understand the depression.  
All they did was install springs,  
That barely changed anything.

---
### Reflection

This poem exposes the quiet violence of an education system that measures worth through endurance rather than understanding. It captures how ambition slowly mutates into exhaustion, and discipline into self-erasure. Beneath the routine of studying lies a deeper cry: students don’t need tougher systems—they need humane ones.
