# Modern Madness

Welcome to the twenty-first century,  
Where everyone should be in a penitentiary.  
People often engage in adultery,  
And ignore every useful advisory.

A world where breathing is hazardous,  
And every second there’s a risk of a new virus,  
But people are counting the number of genders,  
And the most secure job is that of a bartender.

A world where a chicken fights an old beast,  
Wins because it was scripted and was on Netflix.  
People do indisciplinary things and call it mental peace,  
Any inconvenience occurs and they call the damn priest.

People have best friends with whom they hang out,  
But behind their backs, it’s them they bitch about.  
People are so selfish, they don’t see their foul,  
If somebody is naive, he’s brain-rotted inside and out.

A world where criminals are glorified,  
Rapists wander free and contest elections with pride.  
At first, people are horrified and riot nationwide,  
At last, they do a retweet and slowly the fire dies.

A new world war is always on the rise,  
Innocent civilians always die, with tears in their eyes.  
Artificial Intelligence is destroying people’s lives,  
I don’t think anyone will survive or thrive.

---
### Reflection

This poem presents a harsh snapshot of a world drowning in contradiction and misplaced focus. It exposes how outrage has become performative, morality selective, and attention easily distracted. Beneath the anger lies fear—fear that humanity is losing its sense of direction faster than it can recognize the loss.
