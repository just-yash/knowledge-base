# Fragments of a Fading Mind

I was never truly complete—  
Just flickers of thought,  
And echoes of feeling,  
Scattered across too many unfinished pages.  

There are names I don’t say,  
Faces I avoid recalling,  
Places I don’t revisit,  
And memories that leave me caged.  

Some days,  
I am the silence  
Between what I feel  
And what I show.  
Other days,  
I am all noise—  
Hoping someone hears  
Something unspoken.  

If I seem silent and distant,  
Forgive me.  
It’s just me,  
Fighting a war within—  
Trying to gather the pieces of myself  
I lost along the way.  

Grief never asks permission.  
It just shows up,  
Knocks on your door,  
And turns your mind into a prison.  
That’s when you need your friends—  
To remind you  
That the key was never really lost.

---
### Reflection

This poem captures the experience of feeling broken without knowing exactly when or how the breaking began. It speaks to the quiet exhaustion of carrying unseen battles and the fragile hope that healing is possible. Beneath the fading fragments lies a gentle reminder: even when we feel lost, we are not beyond finding ourselves again.
