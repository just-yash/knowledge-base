# Death

Silence.  
The endless reign of peace and neutrality.  
Drowning in the infinite ocean of nothingness.  
Breaking the link between the perpetual and the transient.  
Liberation from an agonized body.  
Emancipation of the eternal soul.  
Discontinuation of existence from the unnatural world.  
Expiration of everything it earned or had.  
Termination of wants and wills.  
Eradication of emotions.  
Annihilation of fear.  
Extirpation of life.  
Just emptiness resides.

---
### Reflection

This poem approaches death not as terror, but as a complete erasure of weight, desire, and identity. It frames dying as a silent severance from struggle—a return to pure absence. Rather than mourning loss, the piece contemplates stillness itself, where nothing is demanded and nothing remains.
