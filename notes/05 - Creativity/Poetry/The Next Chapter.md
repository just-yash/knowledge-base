# The Next Chapter

Lived around my parents all the time,  
Now have to live a thousand miles away.  
For the stairs of life that I have to climb,  
Being the potter and also the clay.  

I am responsible for what I do;  
I cannot start doing drugs like a fool.  
Can’t do absurd things and act cool—  
I must burn my every joule.  

Have to work and study hard every day;  
Love is a hunter, and I cannot be its prey.  
No room for my motivation to decay,  
No matter what this two-faced world may say.  
By hook or crook, I’ll find my own way.

---
### Reflection

This poem marks a moment of quiet resolve—the point where dependence gives way to ownership of one’s life. It embraces responsibility not as a burden, but as a forging tool. At its heart, it’s about choosing self-respect over shortcuts and movement over stagnation.
