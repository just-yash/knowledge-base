# Purpose 

> Why does this MOC exist? 
This MOC maps the logic domain — from propositional logic and well-formed formulas to Boolean algebra, K-maps, and predicate logic — connecting mathematical logic to both philosophical reasoning and digital circuit design.

> What problem does it solve? 
Logic notes spanned both pure reasoning (propositions, arguments) and applied digital logic (Boolean algebra, K-maps) without a unified map linking these two threads.

> What does mastering this domain enable?
Mastering logic enables rigorous argumentation, correct application of Boolean algebra to circuit design, and provides the formal foundation for understanding computational theory and AI reasoning.
