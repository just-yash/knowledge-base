# Purpose 

> Why does this MOC exist? 
This MOC maps the Zettelkasten method — the note-taking and knowledge management system used to organise this entire vault — so that the system itself is documented and improvable.

> What problem does it solve? 
The Zettelkasten system was being used without being formally documented inside the vault, making it hard to onboard to, iterate on, or explain to others.

> What does mastering this domain enable?
Mastering the Zettelkasten method enables converting raw learning into permanent knowledge, creating reusable intellectual assets, building a second brain that compounds over years, and turning the vault into a foundation for writing and research output.
