# Purpose 

> Why does this MOC exist? 
This MOC maps the domain of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — from foundational concepts to applied tools — so all AI knowledge lives in one navigable structure rather than isolated notes.

> What problem does it solve? 
Without this map, notes on LLMs, prompts, coding assistants, and context engineering sit disconnected. This MOC makes every idea findable and links AI knowledge to research and project work.

Mastering AI enables building AI-powered products, contributing to research on multi-camera surveillance, and monetising skills through automation and tooling — all core to your goals.
