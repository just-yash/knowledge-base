# Purpose 

> Why does this MOC exist? 
This MOC maps the digital logic design domain — from number systems and Boolean algebra basics to logic gates and combinational circuit design — as the hardware foundation for computer engineering.

> What problem does it solve? 
DLD notes on basics, circuits, and adders existed but were not connected to each other or to related logic/maths notes. This MOC creates the connection.

> What does mastering this domain enable?
Mastering digital logic is required for GATE, underpins understanding of processor architecture, and connects directly to the Operating Systems and Computer Architecture domains.
