# Purpose 

> Why does this MOC exist? 
This MOC maps the finance and economics domain — from microeconomic demand-supply fundamentals to personal finance, compounding, and insurance — connecting financial theory to real decisions.

> What problem does it solve? 
Finance notes on economics, demand/supply curves, personal financial planning, and compounding had no connecting structure. This MOC creates a path from macro concepts to personal application.

> What does mastering this domain enable?
Mastering finance enables pricing Zaffee correctly, understanding unit economics, making sound personal investment decisions, and reasoning about the economics of building a startup.
