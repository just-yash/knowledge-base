# Plastic Stress Whitening

## Physical Phenomenon

### Mechanism of Color Change

- Bending plastic causes structural changes at the molecular level.
- Molecules are stretched and rearrange within the polymer structure.
- This deformation creates microscopic spaces or voids, often referred to as **crazes**.

### Light Interaction

- The microscopic voids change how the material interacts with light.
- Light is scattered differently within these voids compared to the surrounding material.
- This altered reflection is perceived by the eye as white, regardless of the plastic's original color.
- The chemical composition of the plastic does not change during this process.

###### Example

- **3D Printing:** Removing a brim from a printed part often results in white stress marks where the plastic was flexed.
- **Polypropylene:** High tendency to turn white when bent due to high polymer chain mobility.

---
# Summary

- **Stress Whitening:** The phenomenon where bent plastic turns white due to micro-voids called **crazes**.
- **Light Scattering:** Microscopic voids alter light reflection, creating a white appearance.
- **Chemical Stability:** The chemical composition of the polymer remains unchanged during stress whitening.
