## PM Modi's Visit to Israel

- **Historic Address:** Prime Minister Modi became the first Indian Prime Minister to address the Knesset, Israel's parliament.
- **Warm Reception:** He received a standing ovation and was honored with the Speaker of the Knesset Medal, a first for a foreign leader.
- **Controversial Timing:** The visit occurred amidst international criticism of Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu, who faces an international arrest warrant for alleged war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza.
    - Netanyahu is considered an international pariah, with travel restrictions in 124 countries due to the International Criminal Court's warrant issued in November 2024.
    - Many world leaders avoid associating with Netanyahu due to the Gaza conflict, which has resulted in over 70,000 casualties, including 20,000 children.
- **Netanyahu's Objectives:** The visit served as a "masterstroke" for Netanyahu to improve his international image and gain validation from the leader of the world's largest democracy.

## India's Shifting Stance

- **Traditional Stance:** India has historically supported the right to exist for both Palestine and Israel and advocated for a two-state solution. India was the first non-Arab country to formally recognize the Palestinian State in 1988.
- **Current Shift:** PM Modi's visit suggests a move away from this balanced approach, leading to concerns about India's moral standing and its image in Arab nations.
- **Reasons for the Shift:**
    - **Strategic Partnership:** India and Israel are elevating their relations to a strategic partnership, focusing on security, defense production, and intelligence sharing.
    - **Military Ties:** Israel is India's third-largest arms supplier, accounting for approximately 13% of India's total defense imports.
        - India is Israel's number one arms customer, with 34% of Israel's total arms exports between 2020-2024 going to India.
        - Israeli systems are deeply integrated into India's military, including missiles, seekers, radar, sensors, electronic warfare suits, and drones.
        - These systems have been used in operations, such as the Spice GPS, INS kits, Rampage missiles, and Harop kamikaze drones.
        - The Barak 8 surface-to-air missile was jointly developed by India and Israel and is operational in the Air Force, Navy, and Army.
    - **Economic Ties:** Before the Gaza war, bilateral trade exceeded $10 billion annually, with significant tourism. Discussions are ongoing for a free trade agreement.
    - **Hexagonal Alliance:** Netanyahu is proposing a hexagonal alliance involving Israel, India, Greece, Cyprus, and certain Arab and African/Asian countries to counter radical Sunni and Shia axes.
        - India traditionally avoids military alliances, but this "new India" might consider it for strategic advantage in the region, especially given the formation of alliances like the "Islamic NATO" (Saudi Arabia-Pakistan defense treaty, joined by Turkey).
        - However, such an alliance could complicate relations with traditional allies like Iran and Saudi Arabia and potentially involve India in Israeli conflicts.
    - **Ideological Alignment:** The ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and India's right-wing share symbiotic ideological similarities with Zionism.
        - Support for majoritarianism.
        - Validation of a single religion's dominance in a geographical area.
        - Treatment of minorities, especially Muslims, as permanent suspects.
        - Inspiration for policies in Kashmir from Israel's "Gaza playbook," focusing on normaliing violence under the guise of security operations within a democratic setup.
        - Hindu right-wing and pro-government media openly supported Israel after the October 7, 2023, Hamas attacks, often condemning any sympathy for Palestine.
    - **Pegasus Spyware:** The Pegasus spyware case is cited as evidence of Israel providing tools to India to suppress dissent.
        - The Israeli NSO Group's spyware was allegedly used by the Indian government to tap phones of journalists, opposition politicians, lawyers, and civil rights activists.
        - The Indian government has never officially confirmed its use, and the Supreme Court committee's report on the matter was not made public.

## Concerns and Controversies

- **Adani Group's Involvement:** The Adani Group's increasing involvement in India-Israel defense deals raises concerns about corruption and patronage.
    - Adani Elbit Advanced Systems India Limited produces Hermes 900 drones in Hyderabad.
    - In 2022, the Adani Group acquired a 70% stake in Haifa Port, Israel's most important commercial port.
    - The Adani Group holds a 51% stake in a joint venture with Israeli Weapon Industries and PLR Systems, which has a contract to supply 40,000 NG7 light machine guns to the Indian Army.
    - The Adani Group is under investigation in the US, and a 126% tariff was recently imposed on solar products from India, reportedly as a penalty for Adani's non-cooperation in US investigations.
- **India's Moral Standing:** Critics argue that India's close ties with Israel compromise its traditional moral stance and independent voice on international issues, particularly regarding Palestine.
    - India abstained from UN votes on humanitarian truces in Gaza (2023), stopping arms sales to Israel (2024), and a ceasefire resolution (2025).
    - India also hesitated to sign a UN statement against West Bank expansion.
- **Potential for Coercion:** Questions are raised about whether India is being pressured by the US to engage with Israel or if there's a risk of India becoming compromised or influenced by Israeli software, similar to alleged US influence over Israel.
