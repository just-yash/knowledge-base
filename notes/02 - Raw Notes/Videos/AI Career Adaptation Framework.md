# AI Career Adaptation Framework (A.D.A.P.T.)

## Core Idea

- The most important skill for the next decade is **adaptability**
    
- AI transformation is creating:
    
    - New workflows
        
    - New job roles
        
    - New productivity expectations
        
    - New organizational language
        
- Competitive advantage now comes from:
    
    - Learning faster
        
    - Integrating AI into workflows
        
    - Solving problems using AI systems
        

---

# The A.D.A.P.T Framework

|Letter|Stage|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|A|Acknowledge|Accept the reality of AI transformation|
|D|Dabble|Explore AI tools broadly|
|A|Amplify|Master selected tools deeply|
|P|Problem Solve|Build workflows that solve business problems|
|T|Tie Together|Orchestrate autonomous AI systems|

# Essential AI Vocabulary

## 1. System Prompt

### Definition

- Persistent instruction layer given to AI
    

### Purpose

- Defines:
    
    - Role
        
    - Behavior
        
    - Constraints
        
    - Tone
        
    - Task boundaries
        

---

###### Example

```text
You are a customer support assistant.
Speak English and Hindi.
Escalate angry customers to managers.
```

---

## 2. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

### Definition

- AI system connected to external/private documents
    

### Purpose

- Reduces hallucination
    
- Improves contextual accuracy
    

---

### Workflow

```text
User Query
    ↓
Document Retrieval
    ↓
Context Injection
    ↓
AI Response
```

---

## 3. MCP (Model Context Protocol)

### Definition

- Standardized interface connecting AI to external tools/apps
    

---

### Analogy

- USB port for AI systems
    

---

### Enables

- Slack access
    
- Calendar actions
    
- Database interaction
    
- Workflow execution
    

---

## 4. Fine-Tuning

### Definition

- Training a general AI model for a specialized task
    

---

### Conceptual Analogy

|General Model|Fine-Tuned Model|
|---|---|
|General physician|Cardiac surgeon|

---

### Use Cases

- Legal AI
    
- Medical AI
    
- Customer support AI
    
- Industry-specific automation

# Case Study 2 — AI Receptionist System

## Problem

- Handle:
    
    - 1,000+ calls/day
        
    - Multi-language communication
        
    - Appointment scheduling
        

---

## Workflow Design

### Step 1 — Dataset Collection

- Analyze:
    
    - Real customer calls
        
    - Frequent requests
        
    - Complaint triggers
        

---

### Step 2 — System Prompt Engineering

###### Example

```text
You are a medical clinic receptionist.
Speak English, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu.
Handle appointment bookings and rescheduling.
Escalate complaints immediately.
```

---

### Step 3 — Voice Layer

- Tool:
    
    - Sarvam AI
        

### Reason

- Better Indian language naturalness
    

---

### Step 4 — MCP Integration

- Connect AI to:
    
    - Scheduling systems
        
    - Booking databases
        

---

### Step 5 — Escalation Logic

- Detect:
    
    - Complaint keywords
        
    - Emotional signals
        

### Then:

- Transfer to human operator
    

---

## Outcome

|Metric|Result|
|---|---|
|Calls/day|1,000+|
|Fully resolved|70%|
|Human escalation|30%|
|Availability|24/7|
|Response speed|0.8 seconds|
