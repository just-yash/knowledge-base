# Core Idea

> Everyone knows the tools. Very few know how to leverage them properly.

AI advantage is no longer access.  
It is:

- Context
    
- Systems
    
- Workflow design
    
- Research depth
    
- Execution speed
    

---

# Context Engineering

## Difference

### Prompt Engineering

- Writing better instructions
    

### Context Engineering

- Giving AI everything a smart human would need to complete the task properly
    

AI quality depends heavily on context quality.

---

## Example

Task:

- Write an email inviting 10K subscribers to a GenAI workshop
    

Good AI systems will first ask:

- Who is the audience?
    
- Tone?
    
- Workshop benefits?
    
- CTA?
    
- Brand voice?
    
- Pricing?
    
- Target outcome?
    

Without context:

- Generic output
    

With context:

- High-converting output
    

---

# 5 Layers of Context

## 1. Identity Context

Who is the AI acting as?

- Copywriter
    
- Research analyst
    
- Product strategist
    
- Cybersecurity expert
    
- Teacher
    

---

## 2. World Context

What should the AI know?

- Business
    
- Audience
    
- Industry
    
- Goals
    
- Constraints
    
- Current situation
    

---

## 3. Task Context

What exactly should happen?

- Write
    
- Analyze
    
- Summarize
    
- Compare
    
- Design
    
- Research
    

Specificity matters.

---

## 4. Example Context

Show:

- Good outputs
    
- Bad outputs
    
- Desired style
    
- Formatting examples
    

AI imitates patterns extremely well.

---

## 5. Constraint Context

Define:

- Rules
    
- Boundaries
    
- Non-negotiables
    
- Word limits
    
- Tone limits
    
- Formatting restrictions
    

Constraints improve precision.

# Deep Research Systems

## Principle

Treat AI like:

- Research intern
    
- Analyst
    
- Knowledge synthesizer
    

But:

- Context quality determines research quality
    

---

## Perplexity

Strong for:

- Deep research
    
- Multi-source synthesis
    
- Long-form reports
    

Capabilities:

- Reads hundreds of articles/websites
    
- Creates extensive summaries
    
- Faster knowledge aggregation
    

---

## Model Council (Advanced Research)

Workflow:

1. Multiple AIs receive same prompt
    
2. Each performs independent research
    
3. AIs compare findings
    
4. They:
    
    - Argue
        
    - Disagree
        
    - Validate
        
    - Merge conclusions
        

Outputs:

- Better synthesis
    
- Unique discoveries
    
- Broader perspectives

# Social Research Using AI

## Best Tool: Grok

Reason:

- Direct X/Twitter integration
    
- Strong real-time sentiment visibility
    

---

## Example Prompt

“You are an expert social media researcher who analyzes real-time public sentiment and conversations on X. Search X and tell me what people are currently saying about Claude vs ChatGPT vs Grok. Find:

- Top creators
    
- Common complaints
    
- Pricing expectations
    
- Languages people are posting in
    

Give:

- Raw quotes
    
- Handles
    
- Patterns
    
- Not just summaries.”
    

---

## Key Insight

Facts matter.  
Public perception also matters.

Social research reveals:

- Emotional reactions
    
- Trends
    
- Complaints
    
- Market expectations
    
- Brand sentiment
