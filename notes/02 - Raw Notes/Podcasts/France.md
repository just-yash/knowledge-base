# France
President of France on Trump, India, Modi, Tech & Future
 
## France's Technological Position

- France, despite a history of early technological inventions, lacks scale, capital, and appetite for risk in the current global tech landscape.
- The largest tech companies are American or Chinese, often led by Indian-origin CEOs.
- Europe needs to strengthen its single market to provide a domestic market of 450 million inhabitants.
- There's a paradox of abundant savings in Europe not being properly invested due to regulations and an unadapted financing system.
- A "risk-taker" mindset is crucial for innovation and success.

## Sovereign AI for France and Europe

- France has announced a €109 billion investment in AI infrastructure, with over 60% already deployed.
- This investment aims to build data centers in France, which will be used by European players, improving the AI ecosystem.
- While technology often comes from the US and cash from the Gulf (e.g., UAE), the goal is to attract foreign direct investment and transfer technologies.
- Solutions must comply with European regulations.
- This approach avoids simply buying solutions and providing data without protection or tech transfer.
- France is developing its own Large Language Models (LLMs) like Mali, which is positioned as a strong European alternative to US LLMs.

## Opportunities for Young Startup Founders and Students in France

- France offers a unique ecosystem for Indian students and startup founders.
- Access to high-quality universities and high schools (e.g., _Polytechnique_, _Paris Urban_, _Normal Superior_).
- English is widely spoken in academic settings, addressing concerns about language barriers.
- France provides a unique mix of large companies, institutions, and a vibrant startup innovation ecosystem in Europe.
- Emphasis on creativity, culture, art, and cinema as integral to innovation.
- France is a natural entry point to continental Europe.
- French "taste" is highlighted as a capacity for mixing various competencies and arts in innovation and life.

## Moonshot Technology for France 2035

- India's technological leapfrogging (e.g., Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker) is highly impressive, especially in financial inclusion.
- India trains more engineers annually than the US and Europe combined.
- Predicting a single moonshot is difficult as innovation is unpredictable and often involves failures before success.
- The most important aspect is to provide opportunities for disruptive innovation and accept that many will fail.
- If forced to choose, **quantum computing and quantum technology** are considered France's potential leading areas due to unique mathematicians, capacities, and performing startups and labs.
- Innovation is about taking risks and accepting losses; one must be ready to fail multiple times but keep trying.

## Multipolarity and International Relations

- The partnership between India and France is based on shared values like democracy, rule of law, and multilateralism.
- They deliver concrete results through cooperation (e.g., Solar Alliance, IMAC, AI regulation).
- Both countries value diversity and do not seek uniformity.
- A key aspect is not wanting to be dependent on either of the two major global powers (US or China).
- This reflects the "Indo-Pacific approach" – a third way that is not confrontational but seeks independence.
- Strategic autonomy is seen as crucial for strong democracies to ensure their citizens can truly decide their future without external technological or defense dependencies.

## American Leadership and Dollar Dominance

- Respect is a fundamental part of leadership; disagreements should be handled respectfully within democracies.
- Leaders "going backward" in terms of respect and protocol is surprising and sends a negative message.
- The dollar currently dominates the world's financial system.
- A multipolar order is desired, and efforts are being made to strengthen the international role of the Euro.
- This requires being more innovative, competitive, investing more, signing swap lines, and making the Euro a safe and liquid alternative for central bankers.
- While an opportunity exists for the Euro, dollar dominance has not ended yet.

## Optimism and Concerns for the Future

- **Optimism:**
    - Increasing number of well-educated people globally, especially young people with enthusiasm and creativity.
    - This global education level is unprecedented and will lead to better inventions and solutions for challenges like climate change and biodiversity.
- **Concerns:**
    - Lack of a predictable and reliable framework for defense and security.
    - Entering "uncharted territories" with the war in Ukraine, where a permanent UN Security Council member with nuclear deterrence launched a 19th-century-style war.
    - Some leaders seem to disregard international order and the UN charter as common rules.
    - The global race for more defense equipment, arms, and ammunition is worrying.
- The goal is to make France credible in the defense race while simultaneously engaging with countries to build a new framework to limit the misuse of arms.
- The challenge of the task does not scare him but pushes him to be more energetic and action-oriented.
