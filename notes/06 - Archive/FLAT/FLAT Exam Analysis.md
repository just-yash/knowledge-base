# FLAT Exam Analysis

# FLAT Roadmap for Absolute Beginners

|Stage|What To Learn|Why It Matters|Priority|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|Sets, Relations, Functions|Base mathematical language of FLAT|Critical|
|2|Binary numbers + Strings + Alphabets + Languages|Entire subject is built on strings/languages|Critical|
|3|Logic Basics|Helps in proofs and theorem understanding|Important|
|4|Graph Basics|DFA/NFA are graph/state diagrams|Important|
|5|Finite State Machines (FSM)|Simplest automata model|Critical|
|6|DFA|Core foundation of entire subject|Critical|
|7|NFA + ε-NFA|Conversion questions repeat heavily|Critical|
|8|Regular Expressions|Directly connected with DFA/NFA|Critical|
|9|RE ↔ DFA/NFA Conversion|Very high exam frequency|Critical|
|10|Arden’s Theorem|Repeated PYQ topic|High|
|11|DFA Minimization|Common long question|High|
|12|Context Free Grammar (CFG)|Module 2 foundation|Critical|
|13|Parse Trees + Derivations|Important theory + numericals|High|
|14|Ambiguous Grammar|Repeated PYQ|High|
|15|Pushdown Automata (PDA)|Major scoring topic|Critical|
|16|CNF + GNF|Frequently asked conversion questions|Critical|
|17|Turing Machine (TM)|Module 3 foundation|Critical|
|18|TM Design Problems|Very high weightage|Critical|
|19|Decidability + Complexity|Mostly theory-oriented|Medium|
