# Priority Order

## 🔴 Numericals First (15–20 marks guaranteed)

### 1. Elasticity of Demand

- Price elasticity formula : $E_d = \frac{\% \Delta Q_d}{\% \Delta P}$
- Income elasticity
- Interpret: elastic / inelastic / unitary

### 2. Present Worth Method

- Cash flow table problems
- $PW = \sum \frac{CF_t}{(1+i)^t}$
- If PW > 0 → project is viable

### 3. Depreciation — SLM Only

- $\text{Annual Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Cost} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}}$
- Book value at end of year $n$ = Cost − (n × Annual Depreciation)

### 4. Break Even Analysis

- $\text{BEP (units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{Selling Price} - \text{Variable Cost per unit}}$
- $\text{P/V Ratio} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{Sales}}$
- $\text{BEP (sales)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$
- $\text{Margin of Safety} = \text{Actual Sales} - \text{BEP Sales}$

### 5. Compound Interest / Effective Interest Rate

- $A = P(1+i)^n$
- Effective rate : $i_{eff} = \left(1 + \frac{r}{m}\right)^m - 1$
    - r = nominal rate, m = compounding periods per year

## 🟡 Theory Second (Q1 + Short Notes)

### 1. Law of Demand

- Inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded
- **Exceptions:** Giffen goods, Veblen goods, expectations of future price rise, necessities
- Diagram: downward sloping demand curve

### 2. Law of Supply

- Direct relationship between price and quantity supplied
- **Factors shifting supply:** technology, input costs, government policy, number of sellers
- Diagram: upward sloping supply curve

### 3. National Income

- **Definition:** Total monetary value of all final goods and services produced in a country in a year
- **Methods:**
    - Product/Output Method
    - Income Method
    - Expenditure Method
- **Key terms:** GDP, GNP, NNP, NI at factor cost

### 4. Laws of Returns

- **Law of Variable Proportion (Short Run):**
    - Stage 1: Increasing returns
    - Stage 2: Diminishing returns ← producer operates here
    - Stage 3: Negative returns
- **Returns to Scale (Long Run):**
    - Increasing, Constant, Decreasing returns to scale

### 5. Market Structures

|Feature|Perfect Competition|Monopoly|
|---|---|---|
|Sellers|Many|One|
|Price control|None|Full|
|Product|Homogeneous|Unique|
|Entry/Exit|Free|Blocked|

### 6. Cost Concepts

- **Fixed Cost:** Does not change with output
- **Variable Cost:** Changes with output
- **Marginal Cost:** Additional cost of one more unit
- **Opportunity Cost:** Value of next best alternative forgone
- **Total Cost = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost**

# Q1 One-Liners to Memorize

- **P/V Ratio:** Contribution divided by Sales
- **Break Even Point:** Point where total revenue equals total cost
- **Elasticity of Demand:** Degree of responsiveness of quantity demanded to change in price
- **Time Value of Money:** A rupee today is worth more than a rupee tomorrow
- **Present Value:** Current worth of a future sum of money at a given interest rate
- **Depreciation:** Reduction in value of an asset over time due to use or obsolescence
- **Marginal Cost:** Additional cost incurred to produce one more unit
- **Opportunity Cost:** Value of the next best alternative forgone
- **National Income:** Total monetary value of all final goods and services produced in a country in a year
- **Effective Interest Rate:** Actual annual interest rate accounting for compounding
- **Fixed Cost:** Cost that does not vary with output
- **Variable Cost:** Cost that varies directly with output
- **Giffen Good:** Inferior good whose demand increases when price rises
- **Isoquant:** Curve showing combinations of inputs producing same output
- **Inflation:** Sustained rise in general price level
- **GDP:** Total value of goods and services produced within a country in a year
- **Marginal Revenue:** Additional revenue from selling one more unit
- **Law of Diminishing Returns:** As more variable input is added, marginal product eventually decreases
- **Perfect Competition:** Market with many sellers, homogeneous product, free entry/exit
- **Monopoly:** Market with single seller having full price control
