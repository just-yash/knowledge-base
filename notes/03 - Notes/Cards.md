# Cards

![[Pasted image 20260417064208.png]]

- 52 Cards → 4 Suits → 2 Black Suit (Spade & Club) + 2 Red Suit (Diamond & Heart) → 13 cards each suit 
- Each suit → 13 cards → 3 Face Cards (1 King + 1 Queen + 1 Jack) + 1 Ace + 9 Place Cards (2 - 10)

---
###### Example : A card is drawn at random from an ordinary pack of playing cards.Find the probability of its being 
###### a. An ace 
###### b) a six or a heart 
###### c) Neither 9 nor a spade 

Solution :     
a) 4/52          
b) 4/52 + 12/52 = 16/52         
c) 1 - 16/52 = 36/52       

---
###### Q1) Two cards are drawn at random in succession with replacement from a deck of 52 well shuffled cards, probability of getting both 'Aces' is ? 
###### a) 1/169 
###### b) 2/169 
###### c) 1/13 
###### d) 2/13  

A1)  a) 1/169          
$\frac{1}{13} \times \frac{1}{13} = \frac{1}{169}$        

---
###### Q2) From a pack of regular playing cards, two cards are drawn at random. What is the provability that both cards will be kings, if first card is not replaced? 
###### a) 1/26 
###### b) 1/52 
###### c) 1/169 
###### d) 1/221 

A2)  d) 1/221         
$\frac{1}{13} \times \frac{1}{17} = \frac{1}{221}$           

---
###### Q3) Four cards are randomly selected from a pack of 52 cards. If the first two cards are kings, what is the probability that third card is a king ? 
###### a) 4/52 
###### b) 2/50 
###### c) (1/52) × (1/52) 
###### d) (1/52) × (1/51) × (1/50)

A3) b) 2/50
