# Simple Interest and Compound Interest 

---

# BASIC TERMS

- Principal: $P$  
- Rate of interest (per annum): $R\%$  
- Time (years): $T$  
- Amount: $A$  
- Interest: $I = A - P$

---

# SIMPLE INTEREST (SI)

## Formula
- $$\text{SI} = \frac{P R T}{100}$$

## Amount
- $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{RT}{100}\right)$$

## Key Results
- SI per year is constant  
- For same $P, R$: $\text{SI} \propto T$

---

# COMPOUND INTEREST (CI)

## Annual Compounding
- $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T$$

- $$\text{CI} = A - P$$

---

## Compounded $n$ times per year
- $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{R}{100n}\right)^{nT}$$

---

## Continuous Compounding (advanced)
- $$A = P e^{\frac{RT}{100}}$$

---

# IMPORTANT COMPARISONS

## Difference between CI and SI (2 years)
- $$\text{CI} - \text{SI} = P \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2$$

---

## Difference between CI and SI (3 years)
- $\text{CI} - \text{SI} = P \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2 \left(3 + \frac{R}{100}\right)$

---

# SPECIAL CASES

## Growth factor
- $1 + \frac{R}{100}$

## Depreciation
- $$A = P \left(1 - \frac{R}{100}\right)^T$$

---

## Successive percentage change
- $$\text{Net change} = a + b + \frac{ab}{100}$$

---

# TIME & RATE RELATIONS

## If amount doubles (CI)
- $2 = \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T$

## If amount triples
- $3 = \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T$

---

# APPROXIMATIONS (HIGH VALUE)

## For small $R$
- $$\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T \approx 1 + \frac{RT}{100}$$

---

## Rule of 72 (approx doubling time)
- $T \approx \frac{72}{R}$

---

# LOG FORM (USEFUL)

- $$T = \frac{\log(A/P)}{\log(1 + R/100)}$$

---

# COMMON TRICKS 

- Always compare ratios:
  - $\frac{A}{P}$  

- Convert % to decimal early:
  - $R\% = \frac{R}{100}$  

- For CI problems:
  - Think in terms of **multipliers**

---

# CORE FORMULAS SUMMARY

- $$\text{SI} = \frac{PRT}{100}$$

- $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T$$

- $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{R}{100n}\right)^{nT}$$

- $$A = P e^{\frac{RT}{100}}$$
