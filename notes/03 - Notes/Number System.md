# Number System

## Natural Numbers (N)
- A number that occurs commonly and obviously in nature
- start from 1, ends at ∞ 

---
## Whole Numbers (W)
- All the natural numbers with zero (0)
- Starts from 0, ends at ∞ 

![[Pasted image 20260318225337.png]]

---
## Integers (I) or (Z)
- Any number that can be written without a fractional component
- Whole numbers with there -ve counterparts 
- starts from -∞ , ends as ∞ 
![[Pasted image 20260318225525.png]]

---
## Rational Numbers (Q)
- Numbers that can be expressed as a fraction P/Q of to [[Number System#Integers (I) or (Z)|integers]], where the denominator Q ≠ 0
###### Example 
- -2.5 = -5/2

---
## Irrational Numbers (Q' / $I_r$)
- Real numbers that cannot be expressed as a fraction i.e as a ratio of integers. 
- $∴$ written as decimal numbers
- non-terminating, non repeating 
###### Example 
- $\sqrt{2} , \pi{}$   

![[Pasted image 20260318230210.png]]

---
## Real Numbers (R)
- A number that represents a quantity along a continuous line, containing all of the [[Number System#Rational Numbers (Q)|rational]] & [[Number System#Irrational Numbers (Q' / $I_r$)|irrational]] numbers.

---
## [[Complex Numbers]] (C)
- A number that can be expressed in the form of `a + bi`, where `a` and `b` are real umbers and `i` is imaginary unit (iota) that satisfies the equation $i^2=-1$
