# Rate of Interest
- The amount earned by a unit principal per time

## Type of Rate of Interest
### Nominal Rate of Interest ($i_{nr}$)
- The basic annual rate of interest 

### Effective Rate of Interest ($i_{eff}$)
- the actual or the exact rate of interest earned on the principal during a 1 year period

$$i_{eff}= {(1 + \frac{i_{nr}}{m})}^m-1$$
$m$ - number of interest period per year 

---
###### Example : What is the effective rate corresponding to 18% compounded daily? Assume 1 year is equal to 360 days. 

Soln :   
$i_{eff} = {(1 + \frac{0.18}{360})}^{360}-1$ = 0.19716 = 19.72%

---
