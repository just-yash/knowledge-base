# Discrete Mathematics Introduction 
- About 10% of [[GATE]] exam(8 - 10 marks) comes from Discrete Mathematics 
- Discrete Maths is the base of all CSIT subjects. Its applications can be seen everywhere. 

| Subjects                     | Average Marks in [[GATE]] | Difficulty and Lengthiness                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Logic                     | 2                         | Small and Easy                                                                  |
| 2. Combinatronics            | 2                         | Vast subject                                                                    |
| 3. Set Theory & Group Theory | 2 - 4                     | Very Vast Subject but also very Easy                                            |
| 4. Graph Theory              | 2                         | Subject is Vast but for [[GATE]] its medium. This subject can be very difficult |

Best way to do this Subject : 
1. Ask Why and not What
2. The reasoning after every thing should be clear, then you can solve every Q
3. The more you Practice, the more you Understand, the more you learn how to apply. 
4. Discrete Maths is an interesting Subject so you wont have problem studying it
