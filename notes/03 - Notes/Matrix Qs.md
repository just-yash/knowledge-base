# Matrix Qs

###### Q1) if $[C]_{3*3} = [B]_{3*2} [A]_{2*3}$ . Find the no. of multiplications and additions. 

A1) 
no. of multiplication = 3 * 2 * 3 = 18
no. of addition = 3 * (2-1) * 3 = 3 * 1 * 3 = 9

---
###### Q2) Find $AB$ for two non-null matrices $A$ and $B$. 
$$A=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
1 & 1
\end{bmatrix};B=\begin{bmatrix}
1  & 0 \\
-1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}$$

A2) $$AB=\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 0 \\
0 & 0
\end{bmatrix}$$
As $AB$ is a null matrix ⇒ $A$ & $B$ are singular matrices

---
###### Q3) if $A_{m*(m+5)}$ & $B_{n*(11-n)}$ and both $AB$ and $BA$ exist. Calculate m, n. 

A3)   m = 3 ; n = 8
if $AB$ exists ⇒ m + 5 = n ⇒ m - n = -5 ….(Eq i)
if $BA$ exists ⇒ 11 - n = m ⇒ m + n = 11 ….(Eq ii)

→ Eq i + Eq ii
⇒ 2m = 6
⇒ m = 3
⇒ n = 8
