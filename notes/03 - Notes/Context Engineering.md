# Context Engineering

- [[Prompt Engineering]] is just writing better instructions 
- Context Engineering = giving AI a smart Human would need to do the job 

###### Example 
- Writing an Email to 10K subscribers inviting them for GenAI Workshop 
- Claude will ask for Qs before writing the email for the context 

---
### 5 Layers of Context 
#### 1. Identity Context 
Who is the AI acting as? 

#### 2. World Context 
What does the AI need to know about your situation, business, audience, etc? 

#### 3. Task Context 
What exactly needs to happen? 

#### 4. Example Context 
What does great output look like? what does a bad output look like? 

#### 5. Constraint Context 
What are the boundaries, rules, non-negotiables?
